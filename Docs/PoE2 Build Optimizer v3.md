# V3+ Future Vision Roadmap

## Vision

Transform the optimiser from:

"Optimise my build"

into

"Guide me from Level 1 to an optimised endgame build"

while keeping the optimiser as the source of truth.

---

## Build Timeline Planner

Goal:

Generate a complete progression path from:

Level 1
    ↓
Endgame

Example output:

Level 1-15
- Recommended skills
- Recommended passives

Level 16-35
- Skill upgrades
- Passive milestones

Level 36-64
- Transition preparation

Level 65+
- Endgame build activation

Level 90+
- Final optimisation stage

---

## Build Transition Planner

Determine:

- Best level to switch builds
- Required attributes
- Required skills
- Required supports
- Required gear
- Required passive milestones

Example:

Recommended transition to
Cast on Crit build at Level 74

Reason:

- Gear requirements met
- Attribute requirements met
- Passive requirements met
- Skill requirements met

---

## League Starter Planning

Support:

- League starters
- Midgame builds
- Endgame builds

Generate recommended upgrade and
transition timelines automatically.

---

## AI Build Guide

Optional feature.

No AI required for optimiser operation.

Requires explicit user authorisation.

AI remains an enhancement layer only.

---

## AI Design Principles

Optimizer
=
Source of Truth

AI
=
Explanation and Planning Layer

AI never performs optimisation.

AI consumes optimiser results and
helps explain them.
